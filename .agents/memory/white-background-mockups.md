---
name: White-background mockups
description: Design Studio canvas and product mockups intentionally use a white background with drop-shadows
---

The POD studio canvas is white (not transparent or grey) for a premium, catalog-like look. Transparent product cutouts get soft drop-shadows so they visually lift off the white background instead of looking flat/pasted. Dark garments need their own dedicated dark photo/cutout asset — a tinted white cutout is not sufficient to stay visible against a white canvas.

**How to apply:** any new product/color combination must ship with an asset (or tint) that stays visually distinct against pure white, not just a color-accurate one.
