---
name: Curved-product default view
description: Why mug/cap/waterbottle open in 3D instead of 2D by default in the Design Studio
---

Mug, cap, and water bottle default to the 3D preview when selected in the Design Studio; other (flat) products default to 2D.

**Why:** these products are cylindrical — their curvature only reads correctly in the 3D view. Opening flat products in 2D keeps precise pixel-level design placement easy; opening curved products in 2D would hide how the design will actually wrap.

**How to apply:** 2D stays available for precise editing on curved products too — only the *default* view differs by product category.
