---
name: Upload auto-fix (brightness/contrast)
description: Uploaded design images get automatic brightness/contrast correction before printing preview
---

When a customer uploads an image in the Design Studio, brightness/contrast are auto-adjusted based on the image's own histogram before it's shown on the product mockup.

**Why:** phone photos and casual uploads are frequently under/over-exposed; auto-correcting makes designs look good on fabric/print without requiring the customer to manually edit the image first.

**How to apply:** this is a one-time adjustment applied on upload, not a live filter — an explicit "revert to original" control does not currently exist.
