---
name: Product-switch auto-fit
description: Design Studio re-fits the design to the new product's print zone when switching products
---

When a customer switches the selected product in the Design Studio, the current design is re-fit to the new product's print zone rather than keeping its previous relative size/position.

**Why:** print zones differ in size and aspect ratio between products (e.g. mug vs. T-shirt vs. cap); preserving relative size from the old product can push the design outside the new product's printable area or make it look tiny/oversized.

**How to apply:** any new product category added to the studio needs its print zone auto-fit wired the same way, not just a static default size.
